The amending engine

The Information Technology Act, 2000 rebuilt from the gazette: the Act as published on 9 June 2000, and every amending instrument read from its own gazette, its instructions carried out in date order. Each change is recorded in the result as an Akoma Ntoso textualMod pointing at the provision of the instrument that made it. The result is then set against India Code, section by section.

Read the Act as computed · India Code's reprint, the text it is compared against, is kept with the original documents.

Where the gazettes and India Code differ

Of 129 sections and Schedules, 64 match India Code word for word and 41 differ only in spelling or spacing (92 places, nearly all OCR of the scanned gazettes). These differ in wording, each checked against the gazette page:

  1. s.36, closing words
    Gazette the reliability of the representations made in clauses (a) to (d)
    India Codethe reliability of the representations in clauses (a) to (d)

    India Code drops “made”. No amending instrument touches these words. Source: IT Act 2000 gazette, p.13; both readings of the gazette (its text layer and indra-ocr) agree; page image viewed.

  2. s.67B, first proviso, clause (i)
    Gazette painting representation or figure is in the interest of science, literature, art or learning
    India Codepainting representation or figure is the interest of science, literature, art or learning

    India Code drops “in”. The text was inserted by Act 10 of 2009 and not amended since. Source: IT (Amendment) Act 2008 gazette, p.12; page image viewed.

  3. s.72, opening words
    Gazette for the time being in force, any person who, in pursuance of any of the powers conferred
    India Codefor the time being in force, if any person who, in pursuance of any of the powers conferred

    India Code adds “if”. The Jan Vishwas Act 2023 amended the end of s.72, not these words. Source: IT Act 2000 gazette, p.20; both readings of the gazette agree; page image viewed.

  4. s.89(2)(g)
    Gazette (g) the manner in which the subscriber shall communicate the compromise of private key
    India Code(g) the manner by which the subscriber shall communicate the compromise of private key

    India Code reads “by which”. S.O. 1015(E) of 2002 changed this sub-section only to renumber a reference ((m) to (n)). Source: IT Act 2000 gazette, p.25; both readings of the gazette agree; page image viewed.

  5. s.77A, numbering
    Gazette 77A. A court of competent jurisdiction may compound offences ... (2) The person accused of an offence ...
    India Code77A. Compounding of offences.–(1) A court of competent jurisdiction may compound offences ... (2) The person accused ...

    The gazette prints a sub-section (2) with no (1) before it. India Code supplies “(1)”. A drafting slip in the Act as published; India Code silently corrects it. Source: IT (Amendment) Act 2008 gazette, p.15; page image viewed.
